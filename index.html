Does the risky part work outside the US?

The leveraged part was re-tested on six other stock markets (1997–2026, with dividends). It beat simply holding the market in only one of them. The plan's extra return is a bet that US stocks stay strong, and it's shown here as exactly that.

The Lab: research grades

A separate, higher-risk research track, kept apart from the plan above: long and short, from 30 minutes to 12 months. Every idea comes from published research and is tested with its design written down first. A/B = good enough to paper-trade, C = watch, D = no edge.

    How it runs

    Automated

    A cloud server runs the rules every weekday at 15:45 New York time and places paper orders through a broker API. A health check runs every 4 hours and alerts my phone.

    Guarded

    A cash account that can never borrow, an allowlist of funds, duplicate-order protection, and a kill switch. The engine was replayed day by day over 10 years and reviewed line by line.

    Pre-registered

    Every test's design is written down and saved before it runs, and every result is logged, including the failures, so nothing can be quietly cherry-picked afterwards.

     
    Paper money only. Past and simulated results don't predict future returns. Nothing here is investment advice.
